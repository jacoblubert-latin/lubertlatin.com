FAQ

Common questions

How does scheduling work?

You choose a time on my online calendar, and regular students keep a standing weekly slot. I’m on Arizona time, which doesn’t observe daylight saving time. The calendar shows open times in your own time zone automatically.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged.

Which textbooks do you work with?

I’ve taught from Ecce Romani and Henle and tutored from Wheelock’s and Latin for the New Millennium. I’m also glad to work alongside Memoria Press, Latin for Children, Cambridge, or Lingua Latina — any sequence a student is in.

Do you work with adults?

Yes. I work with adult learners and self-studiers, whether you’re picking Latin back up years after school or working through a textbook on your own. College and graduate students preparing for reading exams are welcome too.

What happens in the free consultation?

It’s a no-obligation 20-minute Zoom call. We cover the student’s current level and goals, and I recommend a plan. Payment details are sent after your consultation.
